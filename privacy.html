Privacy

Draft · last updated 12 September 2026

Draft for the owner to review. Every statement below is written from what the apps visibly do; confirm each one against the actual code and Firebase rules before publishing, and remove this box.

Who we are

Nifra World is an independent app studio in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Contact: nifraworld@gmail.com.

Spelling Buddy

  • Spelling Buddy does not ask you to create an account.
  • Child profiles, progress, stars and streaks are stored only on your device (browser storage). We do not receive them.
  • Word lists you export or import are files you control.
  • The app uses your device's built-in speech voices; no audio is sent to us.

Boithok Ghor

  • You sign in with an account. Group content (tasks, notes, money entries, medical records, photos, contacts, plans, messages) is stored with Google Firebase so that the people you invite can see it on their own devices.
  • Only members of a group can read that group's data. A group manager can restrict who may add or view money and medical entries.
  • Photos are compressed on your device before upload.
  • You can delete entries; deleted items go to a recycle bin first. To delete an account or a whole group, email us and we will do it within 30 days.
  • Medical records are sensitive. Please only add what your family agrees to share.

Khela Ghor

  • You sign in with an account. Matches, players, teams and rankings are stored with Google Firebase so everyone in your group sees the same board.
  • Only members of a group can see that group's matches. Guest players are never counted in rankings.
  • To delete an account or a group, email us and we will do it within 30 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not show ads.

Analytics on nifraworld.com

This website may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (page views and country only) to understand which pages are read.

Children

Spelling Buddy is made for children and collects no personal information from them. Boithok Ghor accounts should be created by adults.
