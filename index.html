Spelling Buddy

spelling.nifraworld.com

শোনো, লেখো, তারা জেতো।

A patient spelling coach for children. It says the word aloud, the child types it, and the exercise book shows exactly which letter went wrong. Word lists follow the school classes, so practice matches what is due on Sunday.

  • Word lists by class, English and Bangla
  • Listen-and-type dictation with a clear voice
  • Beat the Clock speed rounds
  • Stars and streaks that reward showing up
  • A profile for each child in the house
  • Parent / Teacher zone with its own key
  • Share word lists as a file with other families
  • Works fully offline once opened

Why Nifra

Four things every Nifra app promises.

Works when the network doesn't

Open it once and it stays on your phone. Load-shedding, a dead spot on the bus, no data left — practice and records still open.

বাংলা and English, side by side

Bangla letterforms are bundled so যুক্তাক্ষর look right on every phone, and you switch language with one tap.

Built for real phones

Designed on a 540-pixel screen for budget Androids. Small pages, compressed photos, nothing that eats your data pack.

Private by shape

A child's profile stays on the device. A family's room is invitation-only, with a manager who approves sensitive entries.

Install

Put it on the home screen in under a minute.

There is nothing to download from a store. The app you open in the browser is the app — installing just gives it an icon and lets it open full-screen.

  1. Open the app's address in your browser

    On Android use Chrome; on iPhone use Safari. Go to spelling.nifraworld.com or boithokghor.nifraworld.com.

  2. Find "Add to Home Screen"

    Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. iPhone: tap the Share button ⬆, then Add to Home Screen.

  3. Open it from the icon like any other app

    It launches full-screen, works offline, and updates itself quietly the next time you are online. Nothing else to manage.
